# Product positioning

- Status: Working draft
- Date: 2026-07-30

## One-liner

Trip is not for collecting likes. It is the workspace and keepsake for one journey with the people who were there — from plan, to share, to remember.

社交媒体是演出；Trip 是这一趟旅行的工作台和纪念册。

## Who it is for

Small groups traveling together (friends, partners, family): people who need a shared place for the **full** trip, not a public audience.

## What it is

A **travel collaboration tool** with a gallery that social feeds cannot replace:

1. **Plan** — day-by-day itinerary and maps  
2. **Share** — friends upload without an account; credit by name  
3. **Remember** — masonry gallery, lightbox, Live Photo, original-quality download  

The unit of value is **one trip**, not a forever personal cloud and not a social profile.

## Primary competitor (mental model)

The real rival for attention is **social media** (Moments / Instagram / Xiaohongshu), not Google Photos and not generic “travel apps.”

Users already have somewhere to put pictures. The question is *why put them here instead of posting there*.

| | Social media | Trip |
|--|--------------|------|
| Audience | Semi-public, weak ties, “being seen” | This trip’s companions / close friends |
| Behavior | Curate a few shots, caption, persona | Can be complete, messy, originals, Live Photos |
| Timeline | One post, then it sinks | One journey as a durable container |
| Job | Performance / broadcast | Plan → share with the group → revisit |

Social wins at **visibility**. Trip wins at **finishing and keeping one trip together** (itinerary aligned with photos, mutual download, gallery depth).

These can coexist: **a few photos on social, the full roll on Trip.**

## What we are not

- Not a public content network or growth feed  
- Not “another Google Photos” competing on infinite personal backup  
- Not a generic itinerary SaaS without a shared media spine  

## Implications (keep decisions aligned)

- Messaging: scene and ritual (“this trip’s book”), not feature checklists vs Photos  
- Growth: small-circle / invite / real journeys first; do not promise infinite free hosting  
- Free tier: finite quota (e.g. ~5GB per site or account) is consistent with “tool + keepsake,” not “free social CDN”  
- Native apps: only when mobile web blocks camera-roll / Live Photo / bulk upload — not required for the positioning itself  
- Storage backend (disk vs R2, etc.) is an ops choice; it does not change the product story  

## Success signal

Friends use Trip **during planning and after the trip** (not only as a dump folder once), and prefer it over a chat image flood for the shared archive.
